## 1.0.0

First release.

### Added

- A slot above the offhand for an elytra, shown in the inventory and in the creative inventory's Survival Inventory tab.
- Gliding on the slot's elytra with a chestplate on, with durability loss and a stop at 1 durability, as a worn elytra.
- A worn elytra is used before the slot's.
- The slot's elytra shows as wings on the player's back.
- Shift-click puts an elytra in the slot first.
- Curse of Binding and Mending work on the slot's elytra.
- An elytra icon beside the hotbar.
- With Simple Totem Slot, the two slots share one column above the offhand.
- With Inventory Plus, locks and Reach work on the slot, and the settings are on an Elytra Slot tab in Inventory Plus's settings menu.

Requires MenuKit 7.3.0 or newer, below 8.0.0, and SlotKit 2.0.0 or newer, below 3.0.0.
