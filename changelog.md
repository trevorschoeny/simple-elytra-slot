## 1.1.0

### Changed

- The elytra slot and Simple Totem Slot's totem slot now share one column panel, so they line up correctly together or alone.
- Requires MenuKit 7.4.0 or newer, below 8.0.0, and SlotKit 2.1.0 or newer, below 3.0.0.

### Fixed

- In creative, the column no longer sits half a slot too low when Simple Totem Slot is installed but its slot is hidden. It's centered to the right of your armor in every case now.
